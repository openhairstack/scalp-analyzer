# Medical & legal disclaimer

**Read this before using anything built by Open Hair Stack.**

## What this software is

Open Hair Stack builds **research and educational software**. The scalp analyzer demo, the
follicle-detection models, the datasets, and any planning simulations are:

- research baselines and demonstrations,
- teaching tools for the hair-restoration research community,
- an open benchmark platform.

## What this software is NOT

- **Not a medical device.** Not FDA-cleared, not CE-marked, not any jurisdiction's regulated
  medical product.
- **Not a diagnostic tool.** The heuristic "pattern readout" in the demo estimates density
  ratios; it does not classify anyone's clinical hair loss stage reliably and must not be
  used that way.
- **Not a treatment recommendation.** The graft-planning simulation allocates imaginary
  grafts using simplified assumptions. It is not a surgical plan and no surgeon, physician,
  or patient should treat it as one.
- **Not a substitute for professional care.** Hair loss has many causes. If you are losing
  hair, see a board-certified dermatologist or a qualified hair restoration surgeon.

## Data & privacy

- The demo processes uploads in memory and stores nothing.
- Do not upload photos of identifiable patients. Public clinical datasets stay
  research-only; see the Charter.
- Patient-identifiable data is never merged into project repositories. Violations are
  security incidents (see https://github.com/openhairstack/governance/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
